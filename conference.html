About the Conference

10th BFF Conference · Salzburg · July 10–11, 2026

Overview

The 10th BFF Conference brings together researchers working in Bayesian, Fiducial, and Frequentist statistics, fostering exchange between theoretical developments and practical applications.

The 2026 edition will be held in Salzburg and is organized within the BFF Community: bff-stat.org

Dates & Location

  • Dates: July 10–11, 2026
  • Venue: NaWi – University of Salzburg
  • Lecture Hall: 402 (“Blue Auditorium”)

Social Events

  • Conference Dinner: Sternbräu Salzburg (July 10)
  • Pre-Conference Reception: July 9 (to be confirmed)

Further details will be announced closer to the conference.

Support & Community

The conference may be supported by statistical societies. Logos and supporting institutions will be added once confirmed.
