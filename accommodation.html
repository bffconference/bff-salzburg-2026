Recommended Areas to Stay

  • City Centre / Altstadt: close to restaurants, historic sites, and cultural attractions.
  • Main Station Area: convenient for participants arriving by train.
  • South Salzburg / NaWi Area: closest to the conference venue.

Flights to Salzburg

Salzburg Airport (SZG) offers direct flights to selected European destinations. Availability may vary depending on the season and airline schedules.

Participants travelling from overseas may also consider flying to Munich or Vienna, both of which offer a wider range of convenient train connections to Salzburg.

Travel via Munich or Vienna

For participants travelling from the United States or other overseas destinations, Munich Airport and Vienna International Airport are convenient alternatives. From both airports, Salzburg can be reached comfortably by train.

Via Munich Airport

Munich Airport offers many international flight connections. Salzburg can usually be reached by train via Munich Central Station.

Approximate travel time: 1.5–2 hours.

Via Vienna Airport

Vienna International Airport is another convenient option, with frequent rail connections to Salzburg through the Austrian railway network.

Approximate travel time: 2.5–3 hours.

Tip: Salzburg is well connected by train from both Munich and Vienna. These airports may offer more flexible international flight options than Salzburg Airport.

Booking Advice

July is a popular travel season in Salzburg. Participants are therefore encouraged to book accommodation early.

Hotels in the city centre, near the main station, or in the southern part of Salzburg usually provide convenient access to the conference venue.
