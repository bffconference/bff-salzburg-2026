10th BFF Conference

Bayesian, Fiducial, and Frequentist Statistics

About the Conference

The BFF Conference brings together researchers working in Bayesian, Fiducial, and Frequentist statistics. The 10th edition will take place in beautiful Salzburg, birthplace of Wolfgang Amadé Mozart and Christian Doppler. BFF10 will be directly after the IMS Annual Meeting in Salzburg, so you can easily combine both meetings.

Poster Submissions

Poster submissions for the 10th BFF Conference are now open. We welcome poster presentations on topics related to statistics, data science, machine learning, and AI, particularly those connected to uncertainty quantification and statistical foundations.
