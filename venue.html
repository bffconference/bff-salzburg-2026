Getting there

Public transport

Public transport is the recommended way to reach the NaWi building. The venue is served by several city bus lines with stops within walking distance.

You can use the following link to plan your trip via Google Maps:
Route to NaWi on Google Maps

From Salzburg Hbf (Main Station)

From Salzburg Central Station, the NaWi building can typically be reached in about 15–25 minutes using public transport, depending on the chosen connection.

Common options include taking a city bus towards the south of Salzburg and getting off at a stop near Hellbrunner Straße, followed by a short walk (approximately 5–10 minutes) to the NaWi building. Exact routes and schedules may vary, so we recommend checking current connections via:
Salzburg Hbf → NaWi (Google Maps directions)

By car & accessibility

The NaWi building can be accessed by car via Hellbrunner Straße (Hellbrunner Str. 34, 5020 Salzburg). Parking directly at the building and in the surrounding area is limited, and availability may vary during the conference days.

The NaWi building is equipped with step-free access at the main entrance and elevators inside the building, providing barrier-free access to lecture halls such as Lecture Hall 402 (“Blue Auditorium”). Further accessibility details can be provided on request.
