10th BFF Conference · Salzburg · July 10–11, 2026

Overview

The scientific program of the 10th BFF Conference will include a keynote lecture by Richard Samworth, as well as several invited talks on Bayesian, Fiducial, and Frequentist approaches. All in one room - no parallel sessions.

A detailed schedule will be announced once sessions and speakers are confirmed.
